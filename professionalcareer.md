I have over **20 years of professional experience** in the design, implementation, and operation of irrigation systems, including more than **15 years serving as an irrigation project director** for large-scale agricultural developments. My professional work spans over **25,000 hectares** of irrigated area across tropical Latin American countries.

My experience includes the leadership and technical coordination of **commercial drip and sprinkler irrigation projects** for crops such as sugarcane, plantain, citrus, melon, and watermelon, as well as irrigation and fertigation systems for **protected agriculture and hydroponic production**. These projects covered the full lifecycle of irrigation systems, from hydraulic design and system sizing to field operation, evaluation, and optimization.

Currently, I work as an **active consultant**, supporting irrigation design, hydraulic analysis, system performance evaluation, and decision making through the integration of engineering principles, field-based experience, and digital tools. This professional trajectory provides the applied foundation for my academic and research work in irrigation engineering, soil–water–plant interactions, and digital agriculture.
