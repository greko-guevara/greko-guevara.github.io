# Professional Career

## Overview

I am an Agricultural Engineer with extensive professional experience in the **design, implementation, and operation of large-scale irrigation projects**, covering more than **25,000 hectares** across diverse agricultural systems. My work integrates **engineering principles, applied hydraulics, field-based decision-making, and digital tools** to optimize water use efficiency and crop productivity under tropical and subtropical conditions.

My professional trajectory combines **commercial-scale production projects**, **research-driven design**, and **technical advisory roles**, with a strong emphasis on precision irrigation and fertigation systems.

---

## Large-Scale Drip Irrigation Projects

A significant portion of my professional experience has been dedicated to the **design and management of drip irrigation systems** for extensive commercial crops, including:

- **Sugarcane**
- **Plantain**
- **Citrus**
- **Melon**
- **Watermelon**

These projects involved full-cycle participation, from **hydraulic system design and emitter selection** to **field operation, performance evaluation, and optimization**. Special attention has been given to:

- Dripline hydraulic behavior and pressure distribution  
- Water travel time and system response under varying operating conditions  
- Fertigation strategies tailored to crop demand and soil properties  
- Evaluation of irrigation uniformity and system efficiency  

The accumulated experience across these projects exceeds **25,000 hectares of irrigated area**, primarily under tropical climate conditions.

---

## Sprinkler Irrigation and Pressurized Systems

In addition to drip irrigation, I have worked on **sprinkler irrigation systems** for extensive crops, integrating:

- Pressurized hydraulic network design  
- Pumping system selection and energy optimization  
- Control strategies for automated operation  
- Prevention and mitigation of hydraulic transients (water hammer)

This experience complements my background in **pressurized hydraulics and irrigation system modeling**.

---

## Protected Agriculture and Hydroponic Systems

My professional experience also includes **projects in protected environments**, particularly:

- **Greenhouse irrigation systems**
- **Hydroponic crop production**
- **Closed and semi-closed fertigation systems**

These projects required integrated design approaches covering:

- Precise water and nutrient delivery  
- Water quality management and on-site treatment  
- System automation and operational control  
- Optimization of irrigation schedules under controlled environments  

This line of work bridges **agricultural engineering, digital agriculture, and applied soil–water–plant interactions**.

## Digital Tools and Computational Workflows

Across all projects, I have consistently incorporated **computational tools** to support engineering decisions, including:

- Spreadsheet-based modeling (Excel) for hydraulic calculations and system evaluation

- Open-source workflows for reproducibility and technical transparency  

Many of these processes are documented through **open-source repositories and educational content**, supporting both professional practice and STEM education.
