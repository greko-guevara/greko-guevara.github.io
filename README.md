## 👤 About

I am an **Agricultural Engineer (M.Sc.)** with over **20 years of professional experience** in the design, implementation, and operation of large-scale irrigation systems, and more than **15 years serving as irrigation project director**. My professional work covers over **25,000 hectares** of irrigated area across tropical Latin American countries.

I am currently a **PhD student in Soil Physics** and a **full-time professor at EARTH University (Costa Rica)**. My work integrates applied hydraulics, soil–water–plant relationships, and data-driven approaches to improve agricultural productivity through efficient water use.
