## 👤 About Me

I am an **Agricultural Engineer from Costa Rica** with extensive professional and research experience in **irrigation engineering, fertigation, pressurized hydraulic systems, and digital agriculture**. My work bridges **large-scale agricultural production**, **applied hydraulics**, and **computational modeling**, with a strong focus on improving water productivity and system efficiency under tropical conditions.

I have participated in the **design, implementation, and operation of irrigation projects covering more than 25,000 hectares**, primarily using drip and sprinkler irrigation systems. Currently, I am pursuing a **PhD in Soil Physics**, strengthening the scientific foundation of my work at the intersection of **soil–water–plant interactions and irrigation hydraulics**.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

My academic and professional interests are structured around four core areas:

- **Irrigation and Fertigation for Crop Productivity**  
  Precision irrigation, nutrient delivery, and water use efficiency in tropical agriculture.

- **Pressurized Hydraulic Systems**  
  Design, modeling, and analysis of pressurized irrigation networks, including hydraulic transients.

- **Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Systems**  
  Hydraulic behavior, uniformity, performance evaluation, and system optimization.

- **Hydrology, Climate, and Digital Agriculture**  
  Integration of climate data, hydrological concepts, and computational tools for decision support.

Additionally, I am actively involved in **STEM education and agricultural technology education**, promoting programming, modeling, and digital tools applied to agricultural engineering.
